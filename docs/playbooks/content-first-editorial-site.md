# What Is It

This playbook explains how to structure a site where editorial publishing,
pages, posts, media, and SEO are the primary concerns.

# When To Use

Use it when the site succeeds through publishing quality, discoverability, and
editorial consistency rather than through deep custom-record workflows.

# Step By Step

1. Define the main navigation and publishing structure.
2. Use Pages for stable destinations and Posts for ongoing editorial work.
3. Build reusable presentation through themes and templates where appropriate.
4. Use SEO, analytics, and media hygiene as part of the editorial workflow, not
   as an afterthought.

# Examples

- A company blog relies on Posts, Pages, Media, and SEO with only light Coderso
  usage.
- A publication organizes landing pages and editorial categories before scaling
  content volume.

# Common Mistakes

- Modeling every editorial concern as structured records.
- Ignoring media and SEO quality until late publishing stages.
- Building too many one-off pages without a repeatable editorial pattern.

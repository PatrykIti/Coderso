# What Is It

The Automotive Workshop kit is a starter package for service businesses that
need trust-building pages, service discovery, appointment intent, and practical
contact flows.

# When To Use

Use this kit for repair shops, inspection services, maintenance businesses, and
other service operations where booking or qualified lead capture matters.

# Step By Step

1. Start with the kit if the business offers repeatable services and needs a
   clear booking or inquiry path.
2. Review the included pages, forms, and module recommendations.
3. Decide whether booking should be enabled immediately or after lead-gen
   validation.
4. Customize service records, testimonials, and local trust content after the
   initial apply.

# Examples

- A local repair shop launches with services, contact, booking intent, and
  trust-building sections.
- A workshop without online scheduling still uses the kit for qualified inquiry
  forms and structured service discovery.

# Common Mistakes

- Assuming online booking is mandatory for every workshop.
- Leaving generic service copy in place instead of localizing expertise and
  trust markers.
- Skipping service modeling and relying only on static pages.

# What Is It

The Small E-Commerce kit is a starter package for lightweight commerce launches
that need product structure, collections, conversion-oriented pages, and
supporting forms or trust surfaces.

# When To Use

Use this kit when the business needs a focused catalog and product presentation
workflow without starting from an empty workspace.

# Step By Step

1. Choose the kit when product and collection management are central.
2. Review the product-facing pages, templates, and supporting modules.
3. Customize catalog structure, merchandising copy, and trust content after
   apply.
4. Validate product media, forms, and collection logic before launch.

# Examples

- A boutique catalog launches with a manageable product set and collection
  pages.
- A hybrid business combines service content with a small product offering.

# Common Mistakes

- Treating commerce setup as only a design problem.
- Leaving product structure vague and expecting templates to compensate.
- Forgetting supporting forms, trust signals, and collection strategy.

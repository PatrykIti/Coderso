# What Is It

The Local Service Business kit is a starter package for a single service
provider that needs a complete public website without becoming a marketplace or
multi-provider directory.

# When To Use

Use this kit when the business needs services, proof, FAQ, about, and contact
pages for one provider. It is a good default for repair, home service,
consulting, studio, maintenance, and similar local-service sites.

# Step By Step

1. Choose the kit when the site represents one business, team, or provider.
2. Review the generated service, portfolio, FAQ, about, and contact pages.
3. Customize the service offer and proof sections before launch.
4. Check the public inquiry form and contact path after execution.
5. Add real images, testimonials, pricing, and local trust content before
   promoting the site.

# Examples

- A bicycle repair shop launches services, completed work, FAQ, and a contact
  form without needing a searchable provider directory.
- A local home-service company starts from a full page structure and then adds
  booking or richer forms later.
- A small consulting studio uses the kit for a clear offer, process, proof, and
  inquiry path.

# Common Mistakes

- Choosing the Services Directory kit for one service provider.
- Treating the starter copy as final business content.
- Forgetting to replace generic proof, images, and pricing cues before launch.
- Skipping the public contact-form check after execution.

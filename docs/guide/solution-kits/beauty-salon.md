# What Is It

The Beauty Salon kit is a starter package for appointment-driven businesses that
need services, team trust, social proof, and a polished visual presentation.

# When To Use

Use this kit for salons, studios, or similar businesses where services,
availability, and brand presentation are tightly connected.

# Step By Step

1. Choose the kit if service catalog plus appointment intent define the site.
2. Review the services, team, and engagement-related assets it provides.
3. Tune booking depth based on operational readiness.
4. Customize social proof, media, and service differentiation after apply.

# Examples

- A salon launches quickly with service discovery and lead capture.
- A studio adds booking later but still benefits from the kit’s service and
  presentation baseline immediately.

# Common Mistakes

- Overfocusing on visuals while leaving services unclear.
- Enabling booking before real availability rules are ready.
- Ignoring reviews or social proof even though conversion depends on trust.

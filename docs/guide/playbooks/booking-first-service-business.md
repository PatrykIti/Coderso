# What Is It

This playbook explains how to structure a service business where booking is the
main business outcome and the site must support both customer intent and
operational reality.

# When To Use

Use it when appointment availability, services, and reservation handling are the
center of the product rather than just an optional add-on.

# Step By Step

1. Define services and the resources that can fulfill them.
2. Decide what users must know before they book.
3. Build the booking path and verify slot logic in preview.
4. Add support surfaces such as reviews, FAQs, and forms only where they reduce
   booking friction.

# Examples

- A salon uses booking as the main CTA from the homepage onward.
- A clinic starts with services and practitioners, then uses booking as the
  final conversion step.

# Common Mistakes

- Launching booking before services, resources, and availability are stable.
- Overcomplicating the path before the team understands real booking demand.
- Forgetting that trust and clarity still matter before the final slot choice.

Headless Content Management Systems (CMS) have revolutionized the way digital content is produced and consumed. Unlike traditional CMS platforms that couple the back-end content management with the front-end presentation layer, a headless CMS operates as a back-end only content repository.

Why decoupled architecture matters?

The primary advantage of this architecture is flexibility. Developers can choose their preferred front-end frameworks (like Next.js, Remix, or Nuxt) to deliver the content, while content creators can manage everything in a central hub.

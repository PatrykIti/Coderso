# Code Of Conduct

Coderso should be a practical, respectful, and focused environment for building
a modern modular web platform.

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, behavior, and product outcomes.
- Assume that maintainers and contributors are working with incomplete context.
- Accept constructive criticism and respond with technical substance.
- Keep public discussions safe for people with different backgrounds,
  experience levels, and communication styles.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, customer data, or private security details in
  public channels.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction on scope, security, or conduct.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
review comments, community channels, and project-related events or spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository
owner or maintainer channel. Do not use public issues for sensitive personal or
security-related reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, limit participation, block
accounts, or take other reasonable action to protect the project and community.
Enforcement decisions should be proportional to the behavior, its impact, and
the available context.
